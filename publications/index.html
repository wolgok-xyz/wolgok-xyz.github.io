Bidirectional Temporal Diffusion Model for Temporally Consistent Human Animation

Tserendorj Adiya, Jae Shin Yoon, Jungeun Lee, Sanghun Kim, Hwasup Lim
International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) (2024)
arXivYouTube

We introduce a method to generate temporally coherent human animation from a single image, a video, or a random noise. This problem has been formulated as modeling of an auto-regressive generation, i.e., to regress past frames to decode future frames. However, such unidirectional generation is highly prone to motion drifting over time, generating unrealistic human animation with significant artifacts such as appearance distortion. We claim that bidirectional temporal modeling enforces temporal coherence on a generative network by largely suppressing the motion ambiguity of human appearance.
